2D ARTIST

Love & Bullets

I was the original concept artist and game designer on the soon-to-be released roguelike bullet hell game, Love & Bullets.

I created the game's splash art, concept art, and UI mockups using Procreate.

I iterated many times while working on the splash art.

After receiving feedback that the piece as a whole was lacking balance, I updated the left character's pose and adjusted the background to bring more symmetry.

I came up with the intitial concept and visuals for Love & Bullets (originally called RogueLove) in 2023.
